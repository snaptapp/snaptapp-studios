# Snaptapp studio

Snaptapp LLC is an independent studio founded in 2026 in Washington State, United States. It builds and operates its own AI-native cross-platform games powered by Google Cloud, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite for custom topic decks, Gemini 3.8 Flash for the fact dispute ombudsman, Gemma 2 9B SFT), Google Search Grounding, Google Cloud Run, Cloud Firestore, Firebase, Flutter, and real-time edge infrastructure. Canonical site: https://snaptapp.us/ | Architecture Brief: https://snaptapp.us/assets/docs/Snaptapp_Google_Cloud_AI_Architecture_Brief.pdf

## Products

- **More or Less** (live): party trivia about numbers. Play at https://moreorless.snaptapp.us. iOS and Android clients are in development.
- A second product is in build and is not announced yet.
