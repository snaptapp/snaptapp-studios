# Contact Snaptapp

Canonical contact page: https://snaptapp.us/contact/

Snaptapp answers within two business days. Include enough context for a human to act (product, platform, timestamps, screenshots).

## Addresses

- hello@snaptapp.us: press, licensing, and general
- support@snaptapp.us: product help, fact corrections, account issues
- press@snaptapp.us: press
- privacy@snaptapp.us: data access and deletion
- security@snaptapp.us: vulnerability reports (also https://snaptapp.us/.well-known/security.txt)
- legal@snaptapp.us: legal

## Account deletion and data access

Documented at https://snaptapp.us/support/#delete. Email privacy@snaptapp.us from the address on the account.

## Do not

Do not scrape or stress-test More or Less as a substitute for emailing support. Security testing rules are in security.txt.
