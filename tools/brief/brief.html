Architecture Brief — Snaptapp's first title

A real-time multiplayer trivia game where every generated answer is grounded in Google Search, calibrated by a deterministic math kernel, and audited before it reaches the table.

10players per room
<2 sgenerated round

The short version

8 facts
  • One call per round. Players decide whether the true figure is higher or lower than a believable decoy before the shot clock expires.
  • Grounded by default. Custom topics call Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite with mandatory Google Search grounding; curated rounds serve from a verified fact bank.
  • Receipts, not promises. Every generated round carries a grounding_receipt recording its web queries and support spans.
  • The model never picks the decoy. DDM-4, a deterministic kernel shared by Go and TypeScript, derives every distractor number.
  • Audited before promotion. Gemini 3.8 Flash cross-checks claims against primary sources; a 7-metric, 0.0% redline gate admits facts to Firestore.
  • Disputes get a referee. The Fact Ombudsman runs adversarial Search checks on Cloud Run and evicts debunked facts in real time.
  • Fine-tuned on its own facts. Audited facts export into Gemini SFT corpora and Gemma 2 9B LoRA adapters.
  • Built for rooms. Up to 10 players sync on Durable Object WebSockets with a sub-20 ms shot clock.

When a player disputes a fact

Fact Ombudsman · Cloud Run · adversarial Search

A player report opens an adversarial review: counter-evidence queries run against primary sources, then the Ombudsman issues one of three rulings and acts on it immediately.

VERIFIED_CANONICAL

Claim upheld. The fact stays active and the dispute closes.

→ remains in rotation
DEBUNKED_AND_EVICTED

Counter-evidence wins. The fact is removed and its cache entry evicted.

→ cached rounds invalidated
AMBIGUOUS_UNIT

Units or framing can't be reconciled. The fact is quarantined for review.

→ held out of rotation
