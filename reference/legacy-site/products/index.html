More or Less

Live

One fake number. Is the real answer more or less?

Every round gives you a claim, a number that looks entirely reasonable, and a countdown. The number is wrong on purpose. Your only job is to decide whether the real figure is higher or lower before the clock runs out, then watch the reveal explain exactly how far off you were.

Pick from curated topics or type your own. Covered topics are assembled instantly from a hand-verified fact bank; anything else is generated on demand, grounded against live search, and rejected automatically if the numbers do not survive our guardrails.

Five ways to play

Standard

Five rounds on a topic of your choice, mixed difficulty, with a selectable pace — fast, normal, or relaxed.

Cutthroat

Endless sudden death drawn from the whole fact bank. One wrong call and the run is over.

Daily Challenge

The same five rounds for everyone, same fixed clock, once a day. Streaks carry over.

Versus 1v1

Send a challenge, they play the identical pack whenever they like, and the result comes back to you.

Party Room

Up to ten phones in one room on a synchronised clock. Everyone sees the same round at the same moment.
