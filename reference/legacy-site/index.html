More or Less

Live

One fake number. Is the real answer more or less?

A party trivia game about numbers. You get a claim and a plausible-looking figure, and a shot clock. Guess whether the real number is higher or lower before it runs out. Play solo, run an endless sudden-death streak, take the daily challenge, or put up to ten phones in the same room and let them fight it out over a synchronised clock.

How we build

Four decisions we make on every product

01

Real time is the default, not a feature

Shared sessions run on Cloudflare Durable Objects over persistent WebSockets, so every device in a room reads the same clock and the same state. Latency is a design constraint from the first commit, not something we try to patch in at the end.

02

One codebase, every screen

We build in Flutter and ship the same product to iOS, Android, and the web. A fix lands once. A design system holds across phones, tablets, and desktop instead of drifting into three half-maintained forks.

03

AI only where it is checkable

Generated content is not shipped on trust. In More or Less, hand-verified facts are served first; anything the model produces is grounded against search, then run through deterministic guardrails that reject it outright if the numbers or the logic do not hold.

04

Abuse controls before launch

Player-supplied input hits a deterministic content filter at every entry point. Device attestation and bot checks sit in front of the API, and per-account quotas keep one bad actor from spending the whole room's budget.
