Response times. Support and general enquiries: two business days. Security reports: acknowledged within three business days. Privacy requests: within 30 days, as required by applicable law. If you have not heard back in that window, resend to hello@snaptapp.us — something has gone wrong on our side and we want to know.

Before you write

Things that will get you a faster answer

  • Which product. Right now that is More or Less, but say so anyway.
  • Platform and version. Web browser and version, or the phone model and OS version.
  • What you expected and what happened. One line each is plenty.
  • A screenshot. For anything visual, this saves an entire round trip.
  • Your account email if the issue involves your profile, streak, or match history — but never your password. We will never ask for it.
